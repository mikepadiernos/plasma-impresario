## Focus

- Define refinement criteria for potential calendar, scheduling, contacts, and
  address-book workflows
- Define criteria for readable calendar views, time zones, reminders, and event
  conflict feedback
- Define criteria for contact search, recipient lookup, duplicate handling, and
  account-specific sources
- Define privacy, permissions, and error expectations for provider-backed event
  and contact data
- Describe how potential mail-to-event and contact-to-compose workflows should
  preserve context

## Acceptance criteria

- Calendar and contact handoff remains understandable and context-preserving.
- Provider-backed data has clear privacy and permission expectations.
- Contact lookup and recipient flow remain safe, consistent, and easy to recover
  from.
- Mail-to-calendar experiences remain lightweight and non-disruptive.
