# Reading, composition, and attachment refinement

This section covers message rendering, conversation navigation, compose and reply flows, drafts, attachment handling, and editor usability.

## Focus

- Refine message rendering, conversation navigation, compose and reply flows, drafts, attachment handling, and editor usability
- Preserve draft content and attachments across interruptions and clearly report send failures
- Improve conversation navigation, message metadata, quoted text readability, and keyboard access to reading actions
- Provide clear attachment loading, size, download, open, and error states; request confirmation for risky actions
- Validate reply, reply-all, and forward behavior, including recipient visibility and attachment handling

## Acceptance criteria

- Read and compose workflows remain usable through interruptions and retries.
- Draft data and attachments survive disruption without being silently lost.
- Message rendering remains readable and keyboard-accessible.
- Attachment actions and error states are understandable and safe.
