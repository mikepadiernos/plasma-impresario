## Focus

- Refine settings organization, defaults, workspace preferences, notifications,
  status output, and background-task feedback
- Make global, account, and folder settings distinguishable and explain
  inherited defaults
- Keep settings searchable and validate persistence, reset behavior, and
  unavailable integration states
- Offer per-account and per-folder notification controls with privacy-conscious
  message previews
- Surface sync, rule, and background-task failures with actionable details
  rather than silent status changes

## Acceptance criteria

- Settings are discoverable and legible without requiring deep product
  knowledge.
- Notification behavior remains useful without becoming noisy or invasive.
- Background failures are visible, actionable, and understandable.
- Defaults and overrides remain predictable across app restarts and account
  changes.
